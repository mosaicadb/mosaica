# The order suite: results

Generated by `report.py` from the CSV files beside it. Times are ms per query, one thread on one pinned core: the median over queries of each query's median of its repetitions, then `/` the p90 over the same per-query medians. Under a visible set, faiss filters through an `IDSelectorBitmap` and PDX through a selection vector; `(compacted)` is faiss over a copy of the visible rows alone. Exactness (exact k-NN, threshold) is from `check.py`: no mark means every answer was exact; `~` that some items differ but all lie within the rounding allowance; `WRONG n` counts items beyond it; `?` not checked. Filters: `none` is the whole corpus; `random-p` a random visible set of p% (k-NN and threshold shares are then of the visible set).
