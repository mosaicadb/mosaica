YFCC stage 0: pruning fractions for the filtered-track design (stage2-design.txt, sections 1.9, 3, 5)
Measured 2026-11-13, numpy/faiss, one thread on core 11. All numbers MEASURED unless marked.

SETUP
- Base: first 1,000,000 rows of base.10M.u8bin (HTTP range request; a prefix, not a random sample) with their tags.
- Clusters: 1,000 (faiss k-means, 10 iterations on a 256k sample, one seed), centres rounded to uint8, rows assigned
  to the nearest rounded centre. Sizes: min 178, median 975, max 2,610.
- k = 10. Bounds I1, I2, I3 exactly as in section 1.9 (integers, prune only when bound > threshold).
- Queries: 650 from query.public.100K, stratified by visible-set size V on the 1M prefix:
  one-tag 100 each in V 10-99, 100-999, 1k-10k, 10k-50k, 50k+; two-tag 50 each in 10-99, 100-999, 1k-10k
  (no two-tag query of 3,000 sampled has V >= 10k on the prefix). 12,157,760 (query, visible row) pairs.
- "Large one-tag" = one-tag, V >= 10k on 1M (the design's V >= 100k at 10M): 200 queries, 11,633,664 pairs,
  median V 50,290. Reweighting the strata to the whole query set, these carry 92% of all pairs.
- Thresholds: "true" = true 10th smallest integer distance among visible rows; "seed" = 10th best after scanning
  the two nearest occupied clusters (extended to more clusters until 10 rows were seen: needed in 350 of 650
  queries, 1 of the 200 large one-tag). The seed is never tightened, so a real sweep lies between the two columns.
- s is the share of I1 survivors that pass I2 (the design's cost formula); "all" is I2 alone over all pairs.
- Exact distances checked against int64 numpy on 20 queries: 0 mismatches.

VERDICT against the rule (s <= 0.3 keeps the head stage; otherwise plan for 11-18k QPS)
  As the rule is written (1M rows, true 10th distance, p = 32/48/64) s is 0.75 / 0.55 / 0.35 on the queries that
  carry the pairs, and 0.78 / 0.61 / 0.42 from a realistic seed: above 0.3 at every p, so the 48-dimension head
  is out and the exact plan is 11-18k QPS, with the cut worth about 8% (f_c 0.92).
  Two things sit just outside the rule: a 96-dimension head passes it here (0.09-0.15), and the 10M proxy puts
  p = 64 at 0.18-0.22 and p = 48 at 0.35-0.39. A 64- or 96-dimension head costs a third to a half of the full
  kernel before any saving, so neither reaches the 22-31k the design hoped for from p = 48 at s = 0.2 (ARGUED).

NOT DONE / LIMITS
- 10M was not measured; the k = 1 figures are a proxy. The prefix is not a uniform sample of tags: the reweighted
  pair count is 1.45e9 against 1.89e9 for a tenth of the design's 18.9e9, and 20,564 one-tag queries have
  V >= 10k here against 29,702 with V >= 100k at 10M.
- Float ground truth against integer truth (the last item of stage 0) was not checked; the published ground truth
  was not downloaded.
- One k-means seed, one rotation seed, thresholds never tightened during a sweep, nothing timed.
