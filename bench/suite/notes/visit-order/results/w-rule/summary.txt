The W2 width rule, built as a second mode and timed against d999606 and the stored ratio (99b033c)
===============================================================================================

The build (6f86a1c, branch research/w-rule off the reviewed stored-correction head cbb7c4ec2)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The build stores, from every row's coding error:
  - per group and level: G;
  - per cluster and level: M = B~'S_c B~ and N = B~'G B~ (16 x 16, packed), gamma and U.
  The errors are replayed with the replay's own code (errs, order_dirs and orthonormal, moved from study_w_rule into
  v2/wrule.rs). B~ = B (P'P)^-1 is taken against the coefficients the reader holds, a = P'q with P the stored int8
  directions times their scales. A direction the order state left zero gets a zero column.
- k-NN under WidthRule::W2 reads each index-path cluster's deterministic levels at min(1, psi tau / (dwa sqrt U)),
  where tau^2 = a'Ma + gamma max(0, T - a'Na). The window's sigma supplies a (the reader computes it for a cluster
  without one), and T = D'GD is computed once per group.
- Adaptive::rule picks derived, ratio (the default) or w2; the bindings' set_width_rule and ab_orders.py --width-rule
  set it.
- Store format 13; the forms are in the store hash.

U, the theory session's point 2 (u_check.*)
-------------------------------------------
- Over the 100 benchmark queries' first 20 clusters, rms(h) / |D| at a (cluster, level) varies across queries:

  | dataset | CV median | CV p90 | p90 / p10 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | iNaturalist | 3.5% | 7.2% | 1.08 |
  | yahoo | 1.3% | 3.3% | 1.03 |
  | LAION | 0.3% | 0.7% | 1.006 |

- All of it is dwa / |D| (dwa the reader's query factor, `CQ2::dwa`, a norm of D less the cluster's centre term).
- rms(h) / (dwa rms(r)), with r the stored row norms, is constant to 1e-4 on all three: the half-width is entirely its
  code term.
- So U is the mean squared stored row norm, and the width is psi tau h_row / rms(h) exactly: the replay's form, with
  no query-dependent U.

The forms reproduce the replay (forms_check.*)
---------------------------------------------
- On the replay's 4,000 (query, cluster, level) lines per index, iNaturalist, LAION and yahoo, the stored forms give
  its W2 tau^2 and its gamma to 1e-4 (min and max ratio 1.0000).

What the runs say
-----------------
- **W2 is better where the replay said it would be.**
  - iNaturalist unfiltered: 0.86 of spread at 0.99, against 1.01 for the stored ratio and 0.91 for d999606. So at
    0.99 it now beats the start of the day.
  - arxiv unfiltered: 0.64 / 0.65 / 0.69 against 0.69 / 0.70 / 0.81.
  - Recall at psi 5 rises on both: iNaturalist 0.980 to 0.987, arxiv 0.916 to 0.943.
- **It costs a little on LAION and yahoo unfiltered.**
  - LAION: 0.59 / 0.50 / 0.59 against 0.51 / 0.47 / 0.57, about 16% at 0.9, 6% at 0.95 and 4% at 0.99. Its recall at a
    given psi is lower (0.937 against 0.945 at psi 5), with a third fewer rows past the deterministic levels: W2 is
    narrower there.
  - yahoo: up to 10% at 0.99 (0.45 against 0.41).
  - These are the indexes whose stored ratios are near 1 (median 1.03 to 1.09), where the ratio was already right.
- **The filtered sets are mostly level.**
  - Within about 5%, with arxiv random-1 at 0.99 the exception (0.81 against 0.74, a set read almost all directly,
    where the width acts on nothing).
  - region-25 is level everywhere (within 5%), a little better on arxiv.
- **psi is not more uniform across indexes in the real reader.** Recall at psi 5 spans 0.898 to 0.987 under W2, and
  0.899 to 0.980 under the ratio. The replay's narrower psi range (4.1 to 4.8 at 0.9 kept) does not carry over once
  the stop moves with psi.
- **What it costs:** 23 s of build on iNaturalist (14% of the build), 2.2 KB per cluster, and about 1.1 us per
  index-path cluster at query time (0.5% of an iNaturalist query, 2% of a LAION one).

The centre's term (alr_check.*, study_w2_alr): measured, not changed
------------------------------------------------------------------
- The reader's score error is (qv - alr mv) . (z - z^), where W2 takes D = qv d_l and a = P'q. The variant takes
  D' = (qv - alr mv) d_l, T' = D'GD' and a' = a - alr P'm.
- Both are computed from this index's forms, on the replay's benchmark queries, first 20 clusters, each level.
